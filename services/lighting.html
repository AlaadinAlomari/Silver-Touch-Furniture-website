Lighting

LIGHT IS ARCHITECTURE YOU CAN FEEL

Illuminate Every Moment

Silver Touch Qatar designs and installs advanced lighting solutions that transform the way you experience your space. We understand that light is not merely functional — it is the invisible architecture that defines atmosphere, guides the eye, and elevates every surface it touches.

From warm ambient schemes for private residences to precision task lighting for executive offices, our lighting specialists work alongside our interior design team to deliver fully integrated, layered lighting that brings your space to life at every hour of the day.

Get a Free Consultation

What We Offer

Ambient Lighting

Soft, diffused illumination that fills your space with warmth and comfort — cove lighting, recessed downlights, and indirect LED strips designed to create the perfect background glow.

Decorative Lighting

Statement pendants, chandeliers, wall sconces, and feature luminaires — carefully selected and positioned to become focal points that complement your interior design vision.

Functional Lighting

Task lighting for kitchens, workspaces, and reading areas — high-CRI sources positioned for maximum efficiency and visual comfort, eliminating shadows and reducing eye strain.

Smart Control Systems

Integrated dimming, scene control, and automation — programme lighting scenes for morning, work, dinner, and cinema modes, controlled from a single panel, app, or voice command.

Architectural Lighting

Grazing, uplighting, and accent techniques that reveal the texture and form of walls, columns, and ceilings — turning architectural elements into the canvas for your light design.

Supply & Installation

Full supply, wiring coordination, and professional installation of all lighting fixtures — managed in-house and delivered on schedule as part of your complete interior project.
