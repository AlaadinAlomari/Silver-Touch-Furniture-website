Interior Finishings

Complete interior fit-out from shell to handover — all trades coordinated under one roof with dedicated project management across Doha

Finishings

COMPLETE INTERIOR FIT-OUT

Shell to Showpiece

Silver Touch Qatar delivers end-to-end interior fit-out for commercial offices, retail units, hospitality venues, and high-end residences across Doha. We take full responsibility from design development and authority approvals through civil works, MEP coordination, finishing, furniture supply, and final snagging — so you receive a complete, ready-to-occupy space.

Our dedicated project managers maintain rigorous programme control and transparent reporting throughout. With over a decade of fit-out experience in Qatar's demanding market, we understand local regulations, supply chains, and the exacting quality standards our clients expect.

What We Offer

Design & Space Planning

Concept design, 3D visualisation, working drawings and specification development — translating your vision into a buildable, code-compliant design.

Civil & Structural Works

Demolition, blockwork, screeding, waterproofing, and all civil modifications required to prepare the shell for premium finishing.

MEP Coordination

Full coordination of mechanical, electrical, plumbing, and HVAC works — managing specialist subcontractors to maintain programme and quality.

Premium Finishes

Flooring, wall cladding, ceiling systems, painting, tiling, stone, and decorative finishes — all sourced, supplied, and installed to the highest standard.

FF&E Supply & Installation

Furniture, fixtures, and equipment — procured, delivered, and installed as a turnkey package coordinated with the fit-out programme.

Snagging & Handover

Rigorous snagging process, authority completion certificates, as-built documentation, and full handover pack — delivered on time, every time.
