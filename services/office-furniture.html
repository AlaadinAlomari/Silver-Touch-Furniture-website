Office Furniture

PREMIUM WORKSPACE SOLUTIONS

Workspaces That Inspire Performance

Silver Touch Qatar designs and manufactures bespoke office furniture for corporate headquarters, executive suites, co-working spaces, and government offices across Doha. Every piece is crafted in our workshop from premium materials — from solid hardwoods to high-gloss lacquers and genuine leather — delivering environments that command respect and fuel productivity.

We handle the entire process from space planning and 3D visualisation through manufacturing, delivery, and installation. Our team coordinates with fit-out contractors and project managers to meet tight schedules without compromising quality.

What We Offer

Executive Desks

Custom-made executive desks in solid wood, veneer, glass, or lacquer finishes — sized to your office dimensions with integrated cable management.

Open-Plan Workstations

Modular workstation systems with acoustic screens, shared storage units, and ergonomic configurations for teams of any size.

Conference Tables

Boardroom tables in any shape and size — round, oval, boat-shaped or rectangular — with built-in power and data modules for seamless meetings.

Reception & Waiting Areas

Statement reception counters, branded feature walls, and upholstered seating that makes a powerful first impression on every visitor.

Storage & Cabinetry

Floor-to-ceiling filing cabinets, credenzas, bookwalls, and personal lockers — all coordinated with your overall office palette and spec.

Fast-Track Delivery

Dedicated project managers coordinate manufacturing, logistics, and on-site installation to meet your handover deadline — every time.
