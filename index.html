OUR STORY

Defining the Qatar
Design Landscape

Founded in 2014 in Doha, Qatar, Silver Touch Furniture & Interior Design specializes in office and home furniture manufacturing, interior design, and electrical works. We blend style, trust, and function to deliver spaces that reflect elegance, efficiency, and lasting comfort.

From concept to completion, we provide end-to-end interior solutions tailored to each client's unique vision — combining the finest materials with precision craftsmanship.
