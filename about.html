WHO WE ARE

A Legacy Built on
Craftsmanship

Founded in 2014 in Doha, Qatar, Silver Touch Furniture & Interior Design has grown from a boutique furniture atelier into one of Qatar's most trusted names in premium interior design and fit-out solutions.

Our studio brings together a team of experienced designers, master craftsmen, and project managers who share a singular passion: transforming spaces into timeless expressions of elegance and function.

We specialise in office and home furniture manufacturing, interior design, carpentry works, electrical installations, kitchens, carpets & parquet, curtains, and full fit-out services — all delivered under one roof with end-to-end project ownership.
